Safety first

A pulse only fires when you say so.

Laser safety is paramount. The arming workflow puts a deliberate barrier between a casual screen tap and a live beam near the optics.

Disarmed by default

No pocket-fires. No surprises.

Pulsing is the most powerful - and most hazardous - thing this app does, so it's gated behind an explicit arm step every time.

That means you can keep the app open with your hands near the mirrors and trust that nothing fires until you intend it to.
